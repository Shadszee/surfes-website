At Craftmark Surfaces, we are committed to a sustainable future. Our innovative products are designed to minimise environmental impact, promote healthy living spaces, and ensure long-lasting durability. From sustainable manufacturing practices to eco-friendly materials, we are dedicated to creating a better world.


**H3: A sustainable choice**

Our commitment to sustainability is reflected in the durability, renewability, and repairability of our products. By minimising the need for replacements, we significantly reduce landfill waste. Additionally, our custom-sizing options ensure optimal material utilisation, further reducing waste and enhancing cost-efficiency during manufacture.


**H3: Healthy, sustainable spaces**

Craftmark Surfaces contribute to healthier and more sustainable indoor environments. Our non-porous surfaces resist dirt and stains, reducing the need for harsh chemicals and promoting easy cleaning. With low volatile organic compounds (VOC) content and the absence of harmful chemicals, our products support excellent indoor air quality ensuring safety of our customers, installers and manufacturing team.


**H3: A greener future**

We are dedicated to minimising our environmental impact through sustainable manufacturing practices. By optimising our processes, reducing waste, and using eco-friendly packaging, we strive to reduce our carbon footprint.

**H3: Unrivalled stability**

We manufacture all face-routed doors using a superdense, high-density MDF MR substrate. This material provides superior stability and limits bowing, ensuring your cabinetry remains perfectly aligned for years to come.


**H3: Diamond tooling precision**

To achieve a flawless finish, we use a combination of diamond and carbide tooling. This advanced process eliminates traditional machine marks, leaving you with clean, concise lines and a smooth surface ready for a high-end paint finish.

Unlike melamine or veneers, our polyurethane-painted doors are fully repairable. Even years after installation, the paint can be colour-matched to fix blemishes, ensuring your project looks as good as the day it was completed.

From the classic lines of a Shaker profile to intricate V-groove patterns, our CNC routing capabilities allow for significant design flexibility. We help you create a bespoke look without the limitations of natural wood defects.
