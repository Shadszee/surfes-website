Luna Bianco

Pure white / fine light grey speckle

The Luna Bianco benchtop offers a crisp, luminous aesthetic that brings a sense of expansive clarity and modern freshness to any interior. Its brilliant, pure white base acts as a radiant canvas, significantly brightening a room by reflecting natural light. It provides a versatile, elegant foundation that effortlessly complements both bold accents and neutral palettes.
