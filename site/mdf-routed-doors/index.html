How they are made

Made to stay straight. Made to be fixed.

  • Stable core

    Every face-routed door is cut from superdense, high-density moisture-resistant MDF. It limits bowing, so doors stay aligned for years.

  • Clean lines

    Diamond and carbide tooling leaves no machine marks. Edges come out crisp and the face is ready for a high-end paint finish.

  • Repairable

    Unlike melamine or veneer, polyurethane paint can be colour-matched and touched up years after install, or repainted for a new look.

  • Any profile

    CNC routing gives you Shaker, V-groove, cove or a bespoke pattern, without the knots and grain of solid timber.
